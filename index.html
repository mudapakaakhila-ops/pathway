About Pathway After 10

Project documentation, architecture, ethical principles, and presentation guide for teachers and evaluators.

The Problem We Solve

In India, Class 10 board exams represent the very first major academic fork in a student's life. Lack of clear, unbiased information causes immense stress, resulting in students selecting unsuitable streams or falling victim to misleading private coaching claims. Pathway After 10 provides transparent, supportive, non-commercial guidance.

Technical Architecture

Engineered with pure semantic HTML5, modern vanilla CSS3 design systems, and responsive vanilla ES modules. Zero server telemetry ensures 100% student privacy with browser localStorage. Can run on any static web host or locally without external build tools.

Ethical Principles

  • ✓ No admission or salary guarantees: All figures are labeled as broad estimates.
  • ✓ Non-judgmental guidance: Marks are optional; ITI, polytechnic, and vocational trades are presented with equal dignity.
  • ✓ Complete privacy: No personal details transmitted to any server.

Step-by-Step Presentation Script for Evaluators

1

Introduction & Problem Statement

Start on the Home page. Explain why Class 10 students in India face tremendous confusion and how this portal gives them unbiased clarity across Intermediate, Polytechnic, and ITI options.

2

Demonstrate the 1-Click Persona Flow

Click on one of the sample personas (e.g., 'Kiran' for practical skills or 'Ananya' for tech). Show how the 8 questions automatically populate, compute alignment scores, and present 4 tailored recommendations.

3

Interactive Roadmap & Personalized Marking

Click 'View Step-by-Step Roadmap' on a recommended path. Demonstrate expanding milestones and clicking 'Interested' or 'Need to research'. Highlight how personal roadmap progress updates instantly.

4

Side-by-Side Comparison & Telugu Switch

Open the Compare tool to contrast Intermediate MPC with a 3-Year Polytechnic Diploma. Then toggle the 'తెలుగు' language switch in the header to demonstrate regional language inclusivity.
